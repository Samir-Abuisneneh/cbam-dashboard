## 2. Corridor findings

**Ammonia.** Ningbo-Felixstowe is cheaper in 2026 alone, because UK CBAM has not
started. Halifax-Hamburg leads from 2027 and holds it. This ordering is
identical under all three UK price paths and under both CBAM mechanisms, so it
is the most robust result in the study.

**Hydrogen.** Ningbo-Felixstowe is cheaper in every year on the baseline
`frozen` path and on `desnz`. There is no crossover. On `linked`,
Halifax-Hamburg leads from 2027 instead.

| Path | Ammonia | Hydrogen |
|---|---|---|
| `frozen` | NF, HH, HH, HH, HH | NF, NF, NF, NF, NF |
| `desnz` | NF, HH, HH, HH, HH | NF, NF, NF, NF, NF |
| `linked` | NF, HH, HH, HH, HH | NF, HH, HH, HH, HH |

**Every hydrogen corridor claim must name its price path**, and note that
`linked` is a scenario rather than law. Ammonia needs no such qualification.

## 3. Lock-in

Ammonia, decision year 2026, medium prices, 8% real:

| Treatment | PV Halifax-Hamburg | PV Ningbo-Felixstowe | Breakeven | Regret |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `truncate` | 115.76 | 154.48 | GBP 38.72 | 33.45% |
| `hold_final` | 286.07 | 363.05 | GBP 76.99 | 26.91% |

Units are GBP per tonne of annual contracted volume, not per tonne shipped.

The reversal survives both beyond-horizon treatments in the same year and the
same direction, so it is not an artefact of the extrapolation assumption. Only
the magnitude moves. No switching cost is claimed: the finding is the threshold,
and `docs/switching_cost_evidence.md` argues that real corridor-specific sunk
costs plausibly sit above it.

Hydrogen contributes nothing here. Its spot-cheapest and tenor-cheapest
corridors agree in every year.

## 4. Caveats that travel with these numbers

Carry these verbatim into the write-up.

- **Green ammonia on the UK corridor at 2030 is `marginal`, not `justified`.**
  Abatement cost EUR 57.31 against a carbon price of EUR 57.90, a 1% margin. It
  is the only row anywhere in the matrix that trips the marginal band.
- **Two production-cost gaps span separate studies**, Canada hydrogen and China
  ammonia. Every verdict holds its sign under an independent IEA sourcing and
  under both IEA green routes; see `abatement_source_robustness.csv`.
- **The CSCF is assumed, not sourced.** Held at 1.0 because it was 100% across
  2021-2025 and no 2026 value was published as of 8 August 2026.
  `CBAM_CSCF_IS_SOURCED` is False and the outputs carry the flag.
- **Conversion and freight are a declared scope boundary**, not a pending input.
  Both are pathway-invariant, so they cancel out of every within-corridor
  comparison the study makes.
- **The UK CBAM direct-only rule is not represented.** UK CBAM charges direct
  emissions only until 2029, and the model applies full embedded from 2027. The
  effect on the primary scenario is zero for hydrogen, which has no indirect
  default value at all, and 3.5% to 4.4% for ammonia in two years. The rule
  itself was sourced from law-firm briefings rather than primary HMRC text.
- **The hydrogen vessel is a counterfactual.** An ammonia carrier cannot
  physically hold liquid hydrogen. Holding the vessel constant isolates cargo
  density; it is not a shipping option available today.

## 5. Findings that died on 8 August 2026

Recorded explicitly because all three are quoted in earlier documents and in the
supervisor meeting notes. None may be requoted.

| Finding | Status |
|---|---|
| Hydrogen's corridor ordering inverts, flipping to Halifax-Hamburg in 2027 and back in 2028 | **Dead.** No crossover at all on the baseline path |
| The two products give opposed lock-in advice under one regime | **Dead.** Hydrogen has no reversal; lock-in is ammonia-only |
| Absolute cost and competitive exposure point in opposite directions | **Dead.** The two rankings agree in every product-year |

All three were artefacts of shielding hydrogen with a benchmark 56.8% higher
than the law allows. The correction is documented in
`config/regulatory_constants.py` under `CBAM_BENCHMARK_TCO2E_PER_TONNE` and
guarded by tests that fail if any of the three returns unnoticed.

The loss is real and worth stating plainly in the discussion: the opposed-advice
result was the most counterintuitive thing this model produced, and Frano
defined contribution as exactly that kind of finding. What replaces it is
simpler and more defensible, but less interesting.
